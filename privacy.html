Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026.

Hale Bulkheading ("we," "us," "our") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect when you visit or use our contact form, and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any project details you enter. We do not use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts on this site.

How We Use It

Contact form submissions are delivered to our business email so we can respond to your inquiry and schedule a site assessment if requested. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties. Form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service, solely to deliver your message to us.

Your Choices

You can always reach us by phone instead of the web form if you'd prefer not to submit information online. To request that we delete information you've previously submitted, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page with a new "last updated" date.
